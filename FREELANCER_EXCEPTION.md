## Additional permission

Each licensor that makes software available with this exception grants an Eligible Freelancer permission to install and use an Unmodified Official Copy of Mae Captions for Permitted Freelance Work, including when the freelancer charges a client for that work.

This permission allows Mae Captions to be used as an internal production tool. It does not allow Mae Captions itself, access to it, or an automated service built around it to be sold or supplied to clients.

## Eligible Freelancer

An **Eligible Freelancer** is one natural person working independently for that person's own clients. The person may operate through a sole proprietorship or single-member business that the person owns and controls, provided no employee, partner, subcontractor, or other worker uses Mae Captions through that business. The business does not independently qualify; its use is permitted only through its natural-person owner while every condition in this exception is met. Another person may perform unrelated administration or manually handle source and deliverable files, but may not access, configure, trigger, monitor, operate, or perform production work through Mae Captions.

An employee using Mae Captions for an employer, a person working as part of an agency or team, and an organization of any size are not Eligible Freelancers.

## Permitted Freelance Work

**Permitted Freelance Work** means personally operating Mae Captions to produce Static Deliverables for a client. It may be performed as a one-time engagement, recurring retainer, or bulk assignment, provided the Eligible Freelancer remains the only operator.

A **Static Deliverable** is a noninteractive caption, subtitle, transcript, translation, localization, document, or media file, including a draft, review copy, revision, or final version. It does not include code, an executable, a hosted service, an API, a login, or a system through which a client can submit jobs.

The client may receive the resulting media or document files. The client may send source files manually by email, file transfer, a standalone upload portal, or a shared drive that is not connected to Mae Captions; the Eligible Freelancer must personally retrieve the files and initiate the work. A similarly unconnected download portal may deliver Static Deliverables. The client may not receive Mae Captions, its source code or binaries, login or interactive access to it, an API backed by it, or the ability to submit jobs to a system connected to it.

**Personally operating** includes direct CLI use and personally starting a private, non-customer-facing batch script on a system and account controlled by the freelancer, including a private cloud virtual machine. It excludes a continuously running service, a client-triggered or unattended customer job-submission system, and another person accessing, configuring, triggering, monitoring, or operating Mae Captions.

An **Unmodified Official Copy** is source obtained from the [official repository](https://github.com/chadmzoghby-alt/mae-captions), or a package or release published there. Building that unchanged source locally, installing its declared dependencies without patching or substituting them, and changing configuration files, glossaries, inputs, or outputs do not make the copy modified. Changing Mae Captions source or packaging, patching or substituting dependencies, or embedding Mae Captions in a wrapper or another program is a modification. A separate script that merely invokes an unchanged official CLI as allowed above is not a modification.

## Conditions

This additional permission applies only while all of these conditions are met:

- Only the Eligible Freelancer operates Mae Captions.
- Mae Captions runs on systems and accounts controlled by that freelancer.
- The software is an Unmodified Official Copy.
- The freelancer does not distribute, sublicense, sell, rent, lease, operate Mae Captions as a hosted service, white-label it, or provide another person with access to it.
- The freelancer does not embed or incorporate Mae Captions into another product, application, platform, managed service, or customer-facing automated workflow.
- The freelancer does not market or offer Mae Captions itself as a service. Advertising personal captioning, translation, localization, or media-accessibility work performed by the freelancer is allowed.
